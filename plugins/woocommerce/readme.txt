Enable certified practitioners and clinics to share 1-tap multi-item product recommendation routines with automatic aggregate monthly attribution.

== Description ==

Practitioner Protocols connects service-driven brands (skincare, functional medicine, physiotherapy, salon) with their recommending practitioners.

* 1-Tap Cart URLs: Add entire multi-product routines to WooCommerce cart with 1 click.
* Printable Handouts: Support patients with in-clinic physical QR code handouts.
* Privacy by Design: Zero patient health records, diagnostic terms, or medical data is collected or stored.
* Clean Attribution: Track monthly aggregate retail revenue per clinic and protocol.

== Installation ==

1. Upload `practitioner-protocols` directory to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory, or upload the .zip directly via WordPress Admin > Plugins > Add New.
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress.
3. Configure your webhook or connect with Practitioner Protocols Cloud.
