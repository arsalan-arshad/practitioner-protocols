# Practitioner Protocols — Marketing Brief (seed for the Marketing project)

_Do not market anything that is not shipped. Never claim features the code lacks._

## Who it is for
1. **Brands that sell through professionals** (skincare/dermatology, physio and rehab products, pet health, sports nutrition, salon and wellness) on Shopify.
2. **The professionals themselves** — who want clients to actually buy what they recommend.

## The one-line promise
"Turn your recommendations into a link or QR code your clients can buy from in one tap — and see which practices drive orders."

## Positioning
- The professional-recommendation model of closed dispensary platforms, **on your own Shopify store**, open source.
- Privacy by design: no client data stored, attribution is aggregate only.

## Claim guardrails (important — health-adjacent)
- Never claim medical benefit, diagnosis or treatment effects; the app lists products, the merchant owns product claims.
- Never claim "HIPAA compliant" or similar certifications. Say "stores no client information by design" — and only while true.
- Do not use competitor names or logos in the product UI; comparisons in written content must be factual and sourced.

## Proof assets to create
- 45-second screen recording: build a 3-product protocol → copy link → open on a phone → Add all → cart.
- A one-page handout sample with a working QR.
- Diagram: what is stored vs. not stored.

## Channels
Shopify App Store listing · Shopify Community / Partners forums · GitHub README · LinkedIn posts aimed at brands with professional channels · direct outreach to brands that already run practitioner programs.

- [ ] Public name chosen and checked
- [ ] Listing copy reviewed for health-claim language
- [ ] Privacy policy matches the real data model
- [ ] At least one reference store (with permission)
